What is Nonogram Sakura?

A relaxing Nonogram puzzle game inspired by the beauty of Sakura.

Puzzle games are often designed around competition, speed or difficulty. Nonogram Sakura takes a different direction — built around slowing down, thinking, discovering and relaxing. Every solved puzzle gradually reveals a beautiful hidden image.

Classic Nonogram gameplay wrapped in a relaxing Sakura-inspired world — turning a traditional logic puzzle into something calm, beautiful and enjoyable to return to, again and again.

Status: Preparing for Launch | Platform: iOS & Android

🀄 Puzzle Tokens

Every mark you place on the grid comes to life with a hand-crafted Japanese-inspired token.

Sakura Bloom

Marks a filled cell — a small cherry blossom blooming across your grid

Dragon's Mark

Marks an uncertain cell — a watchful dragon holding your guess until you're sure

Torii Gate

Marks an empty cell — a quiet gate reminding you where not to place a bloom

👥 Who It's For

Fans of Sudoku, Picross, crosswords and other logic games looking for a new experience

Relaxation & Cozy Game Players

Players who love calm, beautiful and simple games that aren't overly competitive
