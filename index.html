ABOUT VERSA NOVA

Building ideas into real products.

Versa Nova Media & Entertainment is a Berlin-based media and technology company developing digital products and interactive experiences.

We combine technology, design and creativity to turn ideas into products that people can use, enjoy and engage with. Our work spans mobile apps, AI-powered experiences, publishing and games.
