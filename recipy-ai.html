Recipy.AI

AI-Powered Recipe Discovery & Cooking App

Built with Ionic React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB & Google Gemini AI

In Development Cross-Platform

What is Recipy.AI?

Recipy.AI is a cross-platform recipe app powered by AI that turns the ingredients you already have into delicious, personalized meals.

Search recipes by the ingredients sitting in your kitchen, generate brand-new recipes instantly with Google Gemini AI, and follow a guided, step-by-step Cook Mode for a stress-free cooking experience.

Beyond discovery, Recipy.AI learns from your ratings, cooking history and dietary preferences to recommend meals you will actually want to cook — available on iOS, Android and the Web.

Status: In Development | Platform: Web, iOS & Android
